About us

A Florida trading company with nearly 25 years of experience.

JUCCA LLC is a Florida, U.S.A. based international trading company. We focus on purchasing products directly from suppliers in the United States of America and exporting them to Argentina and all the South American markets.

We operate as an independent buyer and reseller. We develop direct commercial relationships with manufacturers and distributors, and we build long-term business opportunities in international markets.

Our business model

We buy, we resell, we export.

Our objective is to establish reliable supplier relationships and develop sustainable international trade.

01

We buy direct

JUCCA LLC purchases products directly from U.S.A. manufacturers and suppliers, acquiring the merchandise in its own name.

02

We resell

We are the purchasing and selling company, responsible for the commercial transaction.

03

We export

We export those products to customers in Argentina and other international markets, and we are responsible for the export operation.

We are not an intermediary or a contact service. We are the purchasing and selling company responsible for the commercial transaction and the export operation.
